# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity, or sexual orientation.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes respectful technical disagreement, constructive feedback, scientific integrity, accessibility, and care for participant privacy and safety.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, fabrication of research data or findings, or publication of identifiable participant information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and will take appropriate corrective action for conduct that violates this code.

To report a concern, open a private communication route with the repository maintainers once one is configured. Do not publish sensitive personal information in an issue.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
