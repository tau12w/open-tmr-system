# Safety and Ethics

## Human-subject boundary

No human-subject protocol is implemented or claimed here. Overnight cue delivery, sleeping-participant stimulation, and olfactory exposure are disabled and prohibited for the demo.

## Required before human research

A future study would require, at minimum:

- applicable ethics or institutional review;
- informed consent or assent as appropriate;
- qualified clinical or research oversight;
- a preregistered protocol and safety stopping rules;
- participant risk and comfort assessment;
- audibility and volume limits established through review;
- privacy, consent, and data-retention controls;
- incident reporting and de-identification procedures;
- independent review of EEG, audio, and olfactory hardware where applicable.

## Data governance

Synthetic data must be labeled as synthetic. Participant data must be segregated, access-controlled, and never inferred from phone-based estimates or demo output. Raw identifiers and analysis datasets should have documented governance.

## Audio safety

No real participant-facing volume or stimulation claim should be made without hardware verification and approved exposure limits. The generated sine markers are illustrative synthetic signals, not recommended TMR cues.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Content and speech interfaces should support transcription, language metadata, playback controls, and accessible result presentation. Future accessibility evaluation should not treat a single voice or dialect as universally effective.

## Current incompleteness

The Chapter-to-Audio Engine, EEG acquisition, sleep staging, olfactory delivery, and sleeping-participant cue delivery are not implemented as validated research functionality.
