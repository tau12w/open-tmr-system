# Literature Review

## Status

This is a maintained placeholder rather than a systematic literature review. It contains no claims of exhaustive coverage and does not establish evidence for a specific protocol.

## Topics to review

1. Educational memory consolidation and retrieval.
2. Auditory TMR designs and cue timing.
3. Sleep measurement and stage-estimation limitations.
4. Olfactory TMR where ethically and methodologically relevant.
5. EEG quality, artifact handling, and transparent missingness.
6. Text-to-speech accessibility and intelligibility assessment.
7. Human-subject safety, consent, and privacy.
8. Reproducibility and preregistration practices.

## Literature-maintenance requirements

Future entries should include source, date, population, method, major limitations, relevance, and a neutral summary. Search dates and exclusion criteria must be recorded for systematic work. Sources must not be cited merely to imply effectiveness.
