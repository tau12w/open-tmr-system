# Research Methodology

## Status

This document describes methods that may be developed. It does not describe a completed or approved human study.

## Demonstration methodology

The current offline demo:

1. constructs a synthetic educational lesson;
2. validates required lesson fields;
3. creates deterministic acoustic marker metadata;
4. generates PCM WAV marker files;
5. associates synthetic cue records with modalities;
6. writes simulated session and validation artifacts.

Demo output is suitable for software testing only.

## Future study methodology placeholders

Future work would need to specify:

- research question and hypotheses;
- participant and inclusion criteria;
- randomization and blinding;
- learning and retention tasks;
- cue timing and modality;
- sleep-state measurement and uncertainty;
- stimulus safety limits;
- primary and secondary outcomes;
- missing-data handling;
- preregistered statistical analyses;
- replication and adverse-event plans.

## Chapter-to-Audio evaluation

If implemented, evaluation should cover text preservation, segment boundaries, timestamp accuracy, provider reproducibility, metadata validity, and accessibility. Audio quality or learner recall must not be inferred from a unit-test pass alone.
