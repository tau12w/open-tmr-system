# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — v0.1.0 foundation

- Structured educational content.
- Deterministic synthetic acoustic markers.
- Offline demonstration.
- Versioned cue and session metadata.
- Safety and scientific documentation.
- Automated tests and CI.

## Phase 2

- Additional local and provider-specific TTS adapters.
- Android export and MacroDroid event import tools.
- Research dataset import/export and quality reports.
- Reproducible descriptive analysis.

## Phase 3

- EEG acquisition interface implementation after verified hardware documentation.
- Wearable data import.
- Signal-quality tooling and transparent experimental sleep-estimation research.
- Multimodal metadata and artifact documentation.

## Phase 4

- Formal research validation under approved protocols.
- Qualified human-subject oversight and ethics review.
- Broader educational memory research.

## Explicitly not implemented

- EEG hardware communication.
- Clinical or EEG-confirmed sleep staging.
- Overnight automated cue delivery.
- Sleeping-participant sound or odor exposure.
- Smartwatch integration.
- Private APIs for Sleep as Android or MacroDroid.
